# Roadmap

## Online alpha

- QR and room-code joining
- Host, display, and mobile player views
- Complete Copperline scenario
- Server-authoritative checks and civic tracks
- Reconnection and 24-hour room expiry
- After-action export

## After two successful playtests

- Revise the three most consequential pacing or agency problems
- Improve facilitator onboarding
- Add an accessible printable after-action report
- Evaluate a second planning scenario

## Deferred

- User accounts
- Built-in voice or video
- Spectator participation
- Campaign progression
- Character creation
- Community scenario authoring
- Commercial licensing or payments
