# PUBLIC — Assignment

## City of Bellwether

### Copperline Crossroads Joint Staff Assignment

Copperline Partners has requested rezoning and public infrastructure support for a 94-acre former industrial site beside the Hearthside neighborhood.

Council will hold a public hearing tonight. The planning team must recommend:

- Approval
- Approval with binding conditions
- Deferral with a defined work program
- Denial with stated findings

Council has directed the team to address:

- Housing supply and affordability
- Displacement risk
- Transportation and possible passenger rail
- School capacity
- Wetlands and flood storage
- Historic Copper Row resources
- Fiscal and legal feasibility

The applicant states that it needs a decision within 45 days.

There is no requirement to preserve the submitted design. There is a requirement to explain the consequences of your recommendation.

# PUBLIC — Applicant’s Submitted Proposal

## Copperline Commons

- 620 homes
- 62 income-restricted “workforce” homes for 15 years
- 90,000 square feet of retail and office space
- 1,050 parking spaces
- A new highway interchange
- Six acres of public open space
- A preliminary trail beside Copper Run
- $1 million toward off-site transportation improvements

## Requested public actions

- Mixed-use rezoning
- Tax-increment infrastructure financing
- Expedited site-plan review
- Municipal support for the interchange

## Known community conditions

- Hearthside contains approximately 1,900 residents.
- Fifty-eight percent of Hearthside households rent.
- Area rents increased 21% during the last three years.
- The school district reports a shortage of 180 seats.
- The wetland inventory is nine years old.
- Regional plans identify Copperline as a possible passenger-rail station.
- Historic Copper Row occupied part of the site and adjoining neighborhood.
- The comprehensive plan supports transit-oriented housing and anti-displacement measures.

# PUBLIC — Development Program

Choose one program as the base recommendation. Apply its effects when the team finalizes the package.

## A. Submitted Applicant Plan

Approve 620 homes, 1,050 parking spaces, the interchange, and the applicant’s submitted affordability terms.

- Implementation Capacity +1
- Political Support +2
- Public Trust −2
- Equity −2

## B. Compact Transit District

Approve approximately 520 homes in a connected, mixed-use district with reduced parking, a possible station reservation, and negotiated protections.

- Political Support +1
- Implementation Capacity −1 unless the team secures both applicant viability and a transportation or rail agreement

## C. Community Stewardship District

Approve approximately 360 homes with extensive community-controlled land, preservation, environmental restoration, and limited public infrastructure.

- Public Trust +1
- Equity +1
- Political Support −2
- Implementation Capacity −1 unless the team identifies an alternative development or funding partner

## D. Defined Deferral or Denial

Do not approve the present development. Establish a time-limited alternative planning and acquisition program.

- Implementation Capacity +1 if the team names a funded work program and responsible party
- Political Support −2
- No automatic Trust or Equity benefit

# PUBLIC — Commitment Menu

Choose four to six commitments. The team may modify these or create its own.

Each commitment must name:

- Responsible party
- Funding or implementation source
- Timing or enforcement point

“Supported” means the team discovered relevant evidence and secured the necessary agreement, funding, or authority. A professional move may also cancel a listed negative effect.

## 1. Durable affordability and applicant contribution

Require at least 18% income-restricted homes for 40 years and a $2 million anti-displacement contribution.

- Equity +2
- Public Trust +1
- Implementation Capacity −1 unless supported by an applicant agreement

## 2. Hearthside stability program

Fund right-to-return protection, rental assistance, home repair, or a community land trust before construction.

- Equity +1
- Public Trust +1
- Implementation Capacity −1 unless a committed funding source and administrator are named

## 3. Connected streets without an interchange

Use a connected local grid, safe crossings, transit priority, and transportation-demand management.

- Public Trust +1
- Political Support −1 unless supported by mobility evidence and agency or public-safety concurrence

## 4. Passenger-rail reservation

Preserve a station site and corridor without promising immediate rail service.

- Political Support +1
- Implementation Capacity −1 unless supported by a rail agreement

## 5. Copper Run restoration

Create a ten-acre restored buffer, verified performance standards, and funded long-term maintenance.

- Public Trust +1
- Equity +1
- Implementation Capacity −1 unless supported by committed funding and an implementation partner

## 6. Copper Row co-stewardship

Establish a six-acre heritage corridor, fund archaeological work, and give the Descendants Council formal authority over interpretation.

- Public Trust +2
- Equity +1
- Implementation Capacity −1 unless supported by a binding co-stewardship agreement and funded survey

## 7. School-capacity agreement

Tie occupancy phases to available seats, reserve a school site, and make a capital contribution.

- Implementation Capacity +1 and Political Support +1 if supported by a district agreement and committed funding
- Implementation Capacity −1 if adopted without them

## 8. Corrected notice and transparent schedule

Disclose any procedural defect, provide legally sufficient notice, and publish a revised decision schedule.

- Public Trust +1
- Political Support −1 unless the correction preserves the lawful deadline

## 9. Local business and workforce program

Provide construction access, technical assistance, affordable commercial space, or workforce commitments for Hearthside residents and businesses.

- Equity +1
- Implementation Capacity −1 unless a funded administrator and enforceable terms are named

## 10. Community implementation board

Create a public board with milestone reporting and defined enforcement authority.

- Public Trust +1
- Implementation Capacity −1 unless staffing, authority, and reporting responsibility are assigned
